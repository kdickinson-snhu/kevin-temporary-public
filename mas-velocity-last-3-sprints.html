Method and sources

Completed ticket with an authored PR means a ticket whose key appears in the title or description of a PR authored by that contributor and whose Jira status reached Done inside the sprint window. Delivery attribution uses GitHub's PR author.login field—not the person who merged the PR—because completed Jira tickets are handed to QA and reassigned after implementation. Each ticket counts once per contributor. Yan's yan-rnp and yee-snhu identities are combined; a genuinely multi-author ticket can appear under more than one contributor.

Current sprint forecast uses the union of active-sprint Jira tickets currently assigned to Kevin and active-sprint tickets linked from PRs authored by kdickinson-snhu. This preserves Kevin's implementation attribution after tickets are handed to QA and reassigned. Merged and open PRs are included; ticket keys are deduplicated. It is reported separately from completed velocity.

Post-QA defect rate uses completed tickets as the denominator and counts distinct original tickets with at least one traceable defect after QA handoff or in a later sprint. Evidence requires a Jira link or explicit ticket reference plus compatible chronology and PR scope. Multiple follow-up defects against one original ticket count once in the affected-ticket rate. Same-sprint requirement corrections, deployment-only promotion gaps, clarifications that require no code change, and follow-ups created before QA handoff are disclosed but excluded. The current sprint is immature and excluded from the rate.
